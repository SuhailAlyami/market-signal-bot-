# Suhail Hunter: evidence-led study of Number (@11Number111)

Research date: 2026-10-17. **Not a replication of a proprietary method**, and **not evidence of predictive accuracy**. Only publicly surfaced posts/retweets were examined; timestamps are often relative, and full original post archive is incomplete.

1. [Reposts compiled on TwStalker](https://wew.twstalker.com/bdrq81001): $SPX hourly resistance 7789, a 15-minute close below 7782, subsequent 7777–7772 support, and explicit 7789–7782 *no-trade zone*.
2. [Same collection](https://wew.twstalker.com/bdrq81001): $SPX 7408, 7294, 7237; important two-hour break of 7214; resistances 7485/7425; analogous NDQ levels; the author says crossing resistance can invalidate the bearish scenario.
3. [Historical reposts](https://www.twstalker.com/bd_hsyn84690): two consecutive hourly candles below a specified level as a condition for further downside.
4. [Time-specific repost](https://ww.twstalker.com/assaf1424): on 2026-09-25 the author mentions two time divisions, 06:00 and 17:30, but no validated timezone or reproducible formula. This is suggestive, not proof of Gann or time-cycle forecasting.
5. [Older profile that publicly claims to be his](https://mobile.twstalker.com/Mr_Fibonaci): @Mr_Fibonaci wrote that @11Number111 is his account. The name suggests Fibonacci interest, **not proof** that Fibonacci calculations generate his targets. Account ownership has not been independently verified.

## Most defensible hypotheses to test
- **Conditional multi-timeframe levels (strongest evidence):** hourly/two-hour resistance and support, followed by confirming candle close and explicit invalidation.
- **Sequenced price targets and no-trade zones (strong evidence):** a broken key level enables the next targets, while congestion ranges are excluded.
- **Time windows (weak evidence):** predeclared dates/times perhaps condition alerts; unknown timezone and uncertain causal role.
- **Fibonacci levels (possible but unverified):** compare observed targets to prior pivot Fibonacci retracements/extensions and measure false positive overlap.
- **Gann/time-price numerical formula (unverified):** do not reverse-engineer a formula from cherry-picked successful numbers.

## Reproducible research protocol
- Collect >=100 *consecutive* posts with original post timestamp and full text/images (including misses). Record later edits/deletions if known.
- Before looking at future candles, parse symbol, original publication time, timeframe, conditional trigger, invalidation, price target, time horizon and ambiguity.
- Treat unconditional multi-day predictions with no deadline as **unresolved**, not wins after arbitrary months.
- Evaluate each claim against contemporaneously available OHLC data with correct exchange timezone; count stops, failures, timeouts and ambiguous cases.
- Compare: (A) multi-timeframe support/resistance closes; (B) A plus Fibonacci pivots; (C) A plus precommitted time windows; (D) random/naive baselines; report out-of-sample hit rate, opportunity rate, time-to-hit and loss proxies.
- When building the SPX direction detector, remember SPX index has no directly executable volume. Use SPY/ES proxy only with explicit labeling.
- Do not issue SPX 0DTE contract strikes/prices without a reliable options quote, spread, IV and expiry.

## Monday operational rule
Do not assume a 300–400 point plunge will occur on Monday. Track bearish *conditions* and send a WATCH only when fresh data validates the break; suppress alerts in closed sessions or if data is stale.
